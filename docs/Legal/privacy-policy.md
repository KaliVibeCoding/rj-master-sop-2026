# Privacy Policy
**RJ Business Solutions**
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction

RJ Business Solutions ("Company," "we," "us," "our") operates a credit repair SaaS platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information in compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA), and applicable federal and state privacy laws.

**This policy applies to:** Our website (rjbusinesssolutions.org), client portal, mobile interfaces, and all related services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Identity Data:** Full legal name, date of birth, Social Security Number (SSN), government-issued ID
- **Contact Data:** Email address, phone number, mailing address
- **Financial Data:** Credit card/bank account information (processed by Stripe — we never store raw card numbers), income information you disclose
- **Credit Data:** Credit report data pulled via MyFreeScoreNow, dispute correspondence, bureau responses
- **Communications:** Emails, SMS messages, call recordings (with consent), support tickets

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, time on platform, browser/device type, IP address
- **Authentication Data:** Login timestamps, session tokens, portal access logs
- **Transaction Data:** Payment history, subscription status, invoice records

### 2.3 Information From Third Parties
- **MyFreeScoreNow (MFSN):** 3-bureau credit report data including account history, payment history, inquiries, and public records
- **Stripe:** Payment status, subscription state, billing events
- **Twilio:** SMS/call delivery status
- **Affiliate Partners:** Referral source, if applicable

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Legal Basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Providing credit repair services | Contract performance |
| Processing payments | Contract performance |
| Sending dispute letters on your behalf | Contract performance |

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 Service Providers (Processors)
- **Stripe:** Payment processing (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant)
- **Twilio:** SMS and voice communications
- **SendGrid / Resend:** Email delivery
- **Click2Mail:** Physical mail/certified mail delivery
- **OpenRouter / Groq / OpenAI:** AI processing of credit data (data processed for analysis only, not stored by AI providers for training — governed by enterprise agreements)
- **MyFreeScoreNow:** Credit report data provider
- **Cloudflare:** Infrastructure, D1 database, R2 storage, Workers runtime

### 4.2 Credit Bureaus and Creditors
We share your dispute information with Equifax, TransUnion, Experian, and individual creditors/furnishers solely for the purpose of disputing inaccurate information on your behalf.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, court order, or government regulation. We will notify you of such requests to the extent legally permitted.

### 4.4 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. You will be notified via email of any such change.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

### 5.1 Storage Location
All data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (SQLite edge database) and Cloudflare R2 (file storage), hosted on Cloudflare's global edge network in the United States.

### 5.2 Retention Periods
| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Active client records | Duration of service + 7 years |
| Credit report data | 7 years (mirrors FCRA reporting limit) |
| Payment records | 7 years (IRS requirement) |
| Dispute correspondence | 7 years |
| Audit logs | 5 years |
| Portal access tokens | 24 hours (auto-expiry) |
| Inactive lead records | 2 years |
| Staff session tokens | 8 hours (auto-expiry) |

### 5.3 Deletion
After the retention period, records are permanently deleted from all production systems. Backups are purged within 90 days.

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 All Clients (Federal Rights)
- Right to access your credit data under FCRA §609
- Right to dispute inaccurate information under FCRA §611
- Right to know what information we hold about you

### 7.2 California Residents (CCPA)
- **Right to Know:** Request disclosure of personal data collected, used, and shared
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal data (subject to legal hold requirements)
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of sale of personal data (we do not sell data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate for exercising CCPA rights

To exercise CCPA rights: Email support@rjbusinesssolutions.org with subject "CCPA Request"

### 7.3 EU/UK Residents (GDPR)
- **Right of Access:** Obtain a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate personal data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent

To exercise GDPR rights: Email support@rjbusinesssolutions.org with subject "GDPR Request"

Response timeline: 30 days for all data requests.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us immediately.

## 9. Security

We implement the following security measures:

- **Encryption:** All data in transit uses TLS 1.3; data at rest encrypted by Cloudflare
- **Authentication:** SHA-256 password hashing, time-limited session tokens
- **Access Controls:** Staff access limited by role; client data isolated per account
- **Audit Logging:** All data access and modifications logged with timestamp and actor
- **Third-Party Security:** All processors must maintain SOC 2 or equivalent certification
- **Penetration Testing:** Annual third-party security assessment

We are not responsible for security breaches caused by your failure to maintain secure credentials.

---

## 10. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you within 72 hours of discovery via email, in compliance with applicable state data breach notification laws. We will provide: nature of the breach, data affected, steps taken, and recommended actions.
