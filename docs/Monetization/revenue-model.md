## 1. Subscription Plans (Core Revenue)

### Plan Structure

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (2 months free) | Target Client |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $99 | $990 | DIY-curious, mild issues (1-3 items) |
| Standard | $179 | $1,790 | Most clients — 3 bureaus, multiple items |
| Premium | $299 | $2,990 | Motivated clients — AI + strategy calls |
| Autopilot | $499 | $4,990 | Hands-free, high-value clients |

### Unit Economics

**Standard Plan ($179/mo)**
- Average client tenure: 8 months
- LTV: $179 × 8 = **$1,432**
- CAC target: < $200
- LTV:CAC ratio target: > 7:1

**Premium Plan ($299/mo)**
- Average client tenure: 10 months (higher engagement = longer retention)
- LTV: $299 × 10 = **$2,990**
- Target: 40% of clients upgrade to Premium by month 3

### Annual Plan Upsell
- Offer annual plan at month 2 or 3 after initial results demonstrated
- Incentive: 2 months free + priority processing
- Target: 25% of clients on annual plans
- Cash flow benefit: Collect 12 months upfront

## 2. Tradeline Packages (High-Margin Upsell)

Authorized user tradelines are legal, legitimate methods to build credit history rapidly.

### Offering Structure

| Package | Tradelines | Term | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 tradeline | 2 months | $199 |
| Accelerator | 2 tradelines | 2 months | $349 |
| Elite | 3 tradelines | 2 months | $499 |
| Custom | 4+ tradelines | Negotiated | Custom |

### When to Offer
- Client score is 600-680 and needs a boost to qualify for auto loan or mortgage
- Client has thin file (< 3 accounts)
- Client is 30-45 days from a key score threshold (680 for auto, 740 for best mortgage rates)
- Our score simulation shows +30-50 point gain from a tradeline

### Margin
- Cost per tradeline slot: $50-80 (from tradeline providers)
- Sell price: $149-199 per slot
- Gross margin: 60-70%

- Real estate agents (clients need credit to buy homes)
- Car dealerships (clients need credit for auto loans)
- Financial advisors and coaches
- Insurance agents
- Mortgage brokers

- Tax preparers

### Affiliate ROI (per referral at Standard tier)
- Standard plan ($179) × 20% = $35.80 per referral
- Premium plan ($299) × 20% = $59.80 per referral
- Affiliates with 10 referrals/month: $358-$598/month passive income
- This makes our program attractive without margin destruction

## 5. LTV Maximization Strategy

### Month 1-2: Deliver Quick Wins
- Utilization optimization = fast score bump (doesn't require disputes)
- First deletion = massive motivation boost
- Retention is highest after client sees first result

### Month 3-4: Upsell Window
- Client is seeing results and trusts the platform
- Introduce tradeline packages when score is 30 points from a key threshold
- Introduce business funding roadmap when personal score > 680
- Annual plan upsell at month 3

### Month 6+: Referral Activation
- Happy clients are your best salespeople
- Activate referral program after 2+ items deleted
- "Who do you know that needs credit repair?" campaign

### Month 8-12: Goal Achieved → Next Goal
- Client reaches score goal → pivot to mortgage roadmap
- "You qualified for a car loan — now let's get you ready for a mortgage"
- Reframe as ongoing financial optimization, not temporary service

## 7. Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring
Always show Autopilot plan first ($499) — makes Standard ($179) feel affordable by comparison.

### Value Framing
Never sell "credit repair." Sell the outcome:
- "This is $179/month to get a mortgage rate that saves you $127,000 over 30 years"
- "This is $299/month to qualify for the car payment that costs $180/month less"
- "This is $499/month to unlock $500K in business credit"

### Risk Reversal
Lead with the 3-day cancellation guarantee on sales calls. Removing fear of commitment dramatically increases close rates.
