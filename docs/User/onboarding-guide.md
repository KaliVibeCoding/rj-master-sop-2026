**Timeline:** 10 minutes

## Step 2: Identity Verification (Day 1-2)

**Why we need this:** Federal law requires we verify your identity before pulling credit reports. This protects you from identity theft.

**Upload via your portal:**
- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Proof of current address (utility bill, bank statement, or lease — dated within 60 days)
- Optional: Social Security card (speeds up bureau verification)

**Upload format:** JPG, PNG, or PDF — max 10MB per file
**Timeline:** Upload immediately; verification completed within 1 business day

---

## Step 3: Credit Report Pull (Day 2-3)

**What happens:**
- We pull your full 3-bureau credit report via MyFreeScoreNow
- Your Equifax, TransUnion, and Experian scores are captured
- All accounts, inquiries, and public records are imported into our system

**You'll see in your portal:**
- Your 3 bureau scores side-by-side
- Total accounts, negative items, inquiries
- Credit history length

**Does this hurt my score?** No. This is a soft inquiry — it does not affect your credit score.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Quick)

**Q: When will I see my first score improvement?**
A: Most clients see movement within 30-45 days after the first dispute round, when bureaus complete their investigation. Significant results typically appear at 60-90 days.

**Q: Can I speed up the process?**
A: Yes — pay down revolving balances below 30% utilization immediately. This can add 20-50 points quickly, independent of disputes.

**Q: What if a bureau says the item is "verified"?**
A: We send a Method of Verification (MOV) demand letter requiring them to provide the specific method and evidence used to verify. If they can't provide it, the item must be deleted.

**Q: Can I dispute directly with bureaus while you're disputing?**
A: Please don't. It can interfere with our dispute strategy and reset investigation timelines. Let us handle it.
