## About Credit Repair

**Q: Is credit repair legal?**
A: Yes. The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA, 15 U.S.C. §1681) gives every American the legal right to dispute inaccurate, unverifiable, or unfair information on their credit report. We exercise that right on your behalf. Credit repair is fully legal and federally protected.

**Q: Can't I do this myself for free?**
A: Yes — you have the legal right to dispute items yourself. You can contact bureaus directly at no charge. However, effective credit repair requires expertise in Metro 2® format compliance, FCRA statutes, dispute strategy sequencing, and follow-up procedures. Our AI analyzes your report with 12 specialized agents and identifies violations most consumers never find. You're paying for expertise, speed, and results.

**Q: What can actually be removed from my credit report?**
A: We can dispute and potentially remove:
- Inaccurate information (wrong balances, incorrect payment history, wrong dates)
- Unverifiable information (bureau can't confirm accuracy within 30 days)
- Accounts reported after the 7-year limit (FCRA §605)
- Collections reported after the Date of First Delinquency + 7 years
- Duplicate accounts
- Accounts not belonging to you (identity theft)
- Metro 2® format violations (incorrect account status codes, payment rating errors)
- Unauthorized inquiries

**Q: What can NOT be removed?**
A: Accurate, verifiable, legally reportable information cannot be permanently removed. This includes legitimate late payments, charge-offs, and collections that are within the reporting window and being accurately reported. Any company that claims they can remove accurate negative items is likely scamming you.

**Q: How long does credit repair take?**
A: Meaningful results typically appear within 60-90 days. Full credit transformation (from poor to excellent credit) typically takes 6-18 months depending on the severity of your situation. Our 90-Day Action Plan gives you a personalized timeline.

## Results and Expectations

**Q: How many points can I expect to gain?**
A: Results vary significantly by individual. Factors include: number and severity of negative items, current score, credit utilization, and how quickly bureaus respond. Common results:
- 20-50 points: Utilization reduction alone
- 30-80 points: Removing 1-2 major negative items
- 100-150+ points: Removing multiple negatives + utilization optimization
We do not guarantee specific point gains. These are realistic ranges based on typical results.

**Q: Why didn't my score go up after an item was deleted?**
A: Credit scoring is complex. Your score is based on multiple factors. Deleting one item may not immediately show a point gain if other negative factors are weighing it down simultaneously. Score movement is cumulative — each improvement compounds.

**Q: My score went DOWN. What happened?**
A: Temporary score dips can happen for several reasons:
1. You opened a new credit account (new inquiry + lower average age)
2. A balance increased on a revolving account
3. A bureau added a new negative item from a creditor
4. MFSN score model updated
Contact us immediately if your score drops — we investigate every drop.

## The Process

**Q: How do you dispute items? Do you send physical letters?**
A: Yes. All disputes are sent via USPS certified mail with tracking — the same method recommended by the CFPB and required for legal follow-up action. Electronic disputes via bureau websites are easier to "verify" and dismiss. Certified mail creates a legal paper trail and forces a proper investigation.

**Q: What happens after a dispute is sent?**
A: By law, the bureau has 30 days to investigate (45 days if you provided additional documentation). They contact the creditor/furnisher, who must verify the information. If they can't verify within the window, the item must be deleted. If they "verify," we send follow-up letters demanding the Method of Verification.

**Q: How many rounds of disputes will it take?**
A: Most clients see significant results in 2-4 rounds. Complex situations (many items, old debts, identity theft) may require 6+ rounds. We continue working until your credit reaches your goal or all disputable items are exhausted.

**Q: Do you handle all three bureaus?**
A: Standard, Premium, and Autopilot plans cover all three bureaus simultaneously. Basic covers one bureau.

## Legal Questions

**Q: Is what you do the same as a "credit sweep"?**
A: No. Credit sweeps are illegal. They typically involve fraudulent dispute methods like claiming identity theft on legitimate debts, or using a new EIN to create a new credit identity (file segregation). These are federal crimes. We only dispute items that are genuinely inaccurate, unverifiable, or reportable in violation of the FCRA.

**Q: Can you remove a bankruptcy?**
A: We can dispute a bankruptcy if there are inaccuracies in how it's being reported (wrong chapter, wrong date, wrong status, still showing after 10 years). We cannot remove an accurate, verifiable bankruptcy that is within the 10-year reporting window.

**Q: What if a creditor sues me while you're disputing?**
A: If a creditor files a lawsuit, contact us immediately. We are not attorneys and cannot represent you in court. We will refer you to a consumer law attorney. You should respond to any lawsuit — ignoring it results in a default judgment.

**Q: Can you help with identity theft?**
A: Yes. Identity theft victims have additional rights under FCRA §605B. We prepare identity theft dispute documentation and coordinate with all three bureaus to place fraud alerts and dispute fraudulent accounts.
