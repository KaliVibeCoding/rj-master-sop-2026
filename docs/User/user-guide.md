## Dispute Rounds

### What is a Dispute Round?

A "round" is a set of dispute letters sent to one or more bureaus at the same time. Each round targets specific items. After the bureaus investigate (30 days), we review results and prepare the next round.

### Viewing Your Dispute History

Portal → Disputes tab shows:
- Round number and date sent
- Which bureau each letter went to
- Tracking number (for certified mail status)
- Status: In Transit → Delivered → Under Investigation → Result Received

### Dispute Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Letter prepared, pending approval |
| Approved | Approved by staff, ready to mail |
| Mailed | Sent via certified USPS mail |
| In Transit | USPS tracking shows in transit |
| Delivered | Confirmed delivered to bureau |
| Under Investigation | Bureau has 30 days to respond |
| Deleted | Item removed from your report ✓ |
| Updated | Item modified (not deleted, but improved) |
| Verified | Bureau claims it's accurate (we escalate) |
| Escalated | MOV demand or CFPB complaint filed |

## Uploading Documents

**When to upload documents:**
1. ID and proof of address (at onboarding — required)
2. Bureau response letters (when they arrive in your mailbox)
3. Account statements (when requested by staff)
4. Any correspondence from creditors or collectors

**How to upload:**
1. Portal → Documents tab
2. Click "Upload Document"
3. Select file (JPG, PNG, or PDF — max 10MB)
4. Select document type from dropdown
5. Add note if needed
6. Click Upload

**Response time after upload:** Staff reviews within 1 business day and will contact you with next steps.

## What To Do When Bureau Mail Arrives

When you receive letters from Equifax, TransUnion, or Experian:

1. **Don't ignore it** — These are time-sensitive
2. **Take a clear photo or scan** of every page
3. **Upload immediately** to your portal (Documents → Upload)
4. **Label it correctly:** "Bureau Response — [Bureau Name] — [Date]"
5. **Email us a heads-up:** support@rjbusinesssolutions.org

We will review the response and prepare next steps within 2 business days.

**Common bureau responses and what they mean:**

| Bureau Says | What It Means | Our Next Step |
|---|---|---|
| "Item deleted" | Removed from report ✓ | Log deletion; check score |
| "Item updated" | Information modified | Review change; next round if still negative |
| "Verified as accurate" | They claim it's correct | Send Method of Verification demand |
| "We need more information" | Incomplete dispute | Provide requested documentation |
| No response in 35 days | Violation of FCRA §611 | Demand deletion; CFPB complaint |
